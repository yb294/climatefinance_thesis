- Plant ranking follows year and then a sub sector order of → Coal → Oil → Gas
    - Each unique asset gets assigned exactly one rank when it first appears
    - This means the same asset keeps its rank across multiple years for partial phase-outs
    - As a result, the start_rank for a year might be lower than the end_rank of the previous year if plants with lower ranks continue their phase-out from previous years. 
Why does this happen? This is because say there is a Coal plant that is partially phased out in the previous year, followed by a Gas plant that is partially phased out as well; for the next year, the phase out starts from the coal plant. Thus the start rank for the next year is lower than the end rank in the previous year.

- End ranks for a country across criteria are not the same. 
Why? This is because the total number of assets being phased out for the same country in different criteria are different
